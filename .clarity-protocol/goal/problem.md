# Problem Statement

Sarah's personal website (scooley.io) needs to evolve from a minimal placeholder into a multi-purpose personal hub. It currently runs on Jekyll with the Minimal Mistakes theme on GitHub Pages, but has very little content or structure.

## What the site needs to be

- **Blog** — A place to share things she finds interesting. Topics TBD.
- **Projects** — Showcase of work she's done or is doing.
- **Resume** — Professional profile / CV.
- **Rental property** — The family recently got a cabin and may want a section for their rental house (scope and approach still open).

## Why This Matters

This is the single place that ties together Sarah's professional identity, creative output, and potentially a side business (rental). Getting the structure right early means each section can grow independently without the site feeling disjointed.

## Success Criteria

- The site has distinct, navigable sections for blog, projects, resume, and (optionally) the rental property.
- Content is easy to add and maintain (leveraging Jekyll's strengths).
- The site feels cohesive — one person's hub, not four disconnected microsites.
- Visitors can quickly find what's relevant to them (employer vs. blog reader vs. potential renter).
